My research focuses on safety verification of cyber-physical systems e.g., autonomous cars, trains, planes or robots.
More specifically, I develop logics for multi-player hybrid games that allow dealing with situations involving multiple cyber-physical system, as they frequently occur in e.g., traffic. 

I am currently employed as a doctoral researcher at the group *[Logic of Autonomous Dynamical Systems](https://logic.kastel.kit.edu/)* (KASTEL-LADS) led by *[Prof. André Platzer](https://symbolaris.com/)* at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology.
I received my Master's degree in Information Systems from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology where I wrote my Master's Thesis under the supervision of Prof. André Platzer.
